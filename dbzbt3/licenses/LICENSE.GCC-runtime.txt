bt3.aarch64 statically links the GCC runtime libraries libstdc++.a (GCC 7.5.0) and libgcc.a (GCC 7.5.0), taken
unmodified from the Ubuntu 18.04 cross packages libstdc++-7-dev-arm64-cross and libgcc-7-dev-arm64-cross
(7.5.0-3ubuntu1~18.04cross1); port/tools/r36s_toolchain.sh fetches them.
